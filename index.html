How to read the numbers

  • All-offer count includes all 18 explicit pricing records, including recurring options, fees and one promotion.
  • Representative prices use one marked offer per competitor (5 total); the display preserves each offer’s unit.
  • Comparable hardware statistics use only the two posted up-front automatic machine prices ($3,999 and $7,999), excluding monthly fees and deposits.
  • Manual board statistics use the two representative single-board prices ($18.50 and $39.99); bundle offers are not in this benchmark.
  • No cross-unit mean or median is calculated for appliance prices, per-pound services, per-bag services, and fees.
  • “Regular price” is shown only when established in the source data; no compare-at prices were supplied as confirmed historical regular prices.

Verification & limitations

  • Prices, availability and offer details are snapshots from October 27, 2026; they may have changed.
  • Most listed prices are marked Verified in the source dataset; Poplin’s coupon and Weave Isaac 1 optional premium membership are Partially Verified.
  • Weave Isaac products are early-release / pre-order offers with geographic and recurring-payment terms.
  • Poplin pricing varies by ZIP code; Rinse pricing and delivery fees may vary by service area.
  • BoxLegend was excluded from competitor count because the reviewed official product listing was sold out.
  • The $799–$1,299 range is a preliminary venture hypothesis. Competitor research does not establish cost, margin, demand, optimal price, or willingness to pay.
  • Features and positioning describe company claims and the supplied analysis; they are not independent performance tests.
